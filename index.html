The Battle of Goddess of Death & Goddess of Life

The Heavenly Island has become a battlefield between two sisters who were never meant to fight. Where crimson thorns claw at a bruised, storm-lit sky, the Goddess of Death leads the Undead in an assault to claim the island for herself, her curved blade held in a hush of dying embers — every step she takes cracks the earth with the weight of endings. Across from her, bathed in a rising dawn of gold and blue, the Goddess of Life rallies her Valkyrie and Angel host to hold the ground, her crescent staff raised, her halo a quiet promise that nothing is ever truly lost. Choose a side and fight your way through wave after wave — because at the height of the battle, the opposing Goddess herself descends to end it personally.
